Privacy

What this site keeps.

Occuletics is a static site. There is no account and no shopping cart on this server.

Orders

Buy buttons open Square Payment Links. Square processes the payment. Occuletics does not see or store your card number. Square's own privacy policy covers that checkout.

Messages

The contact form opens an email to occuletics@gmail.com, or posts to a form address if one has been configured. We use what you send to answer you. We do not sell it.

Fonts

The site loads Barlow and Barlow Condensed from Google Fonts. Your browser requests those files from Google.
