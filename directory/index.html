Indiana lets the employer or its insurer direct medical care for work injuries (IC 22-3-3-4). Use this directory to build your list of go-to providers. Read the Workers' Compensation Board of Indiana.

For adjusters, TPAs, and carriers, including Travelers, Gallagher Bassett, Sedgwick, and Liberty Mutual: use this as local discovery and a gap-filler alongside your own provider locator. Always confirm network participation.

In Illinois the employee generally chooses their treating doctor (820 ILCS 305/8(a)). This directory helps find work-injury-experienced providers nearby. Read the Illinois Workers' Compensation Commission.
