Before a listing goes live

Occuletics checks licensure before the listing is published. Indiana clinics are checked with the Indiana Professional Licensing Agency. Illinois clinics are checked with the Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation. A DOT certified examiner is checked on the FMCSA National Registry.

Physical therapy capabilities

Indiana and Illinois allow some physical therapy evaluation and treatment without a referral. A work comp claim usually still needs an order from the authorized treating physician. Check claim authorization.
