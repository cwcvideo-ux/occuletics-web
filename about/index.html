The author

David M. Wilson, LAT, ATC

Licensed athletic trainer. Industrial floors since 2002. The posters are the new front of the work.

Occuletics started as on-site industrial ergonomics and sports medicine. Pre-shift stretching for trucking and steel. The idea was simple: people who do physical work are industrial athletes, and they deserve the same kind of preparation a team gets before it plays.

For more than twenty years that work happened in person. A trainer on the floor. A sequence at the start of the shift. Employers who wanted the habit without a contract trainer every morning are the reason the posters exist.

The company is now built to sell clinician-authored ergonomic and workplace-safety posters, printable PDFs, and break-room kits. Industrial, office, and retail. On-site programs are still available in a limited way. They are not the front page.
